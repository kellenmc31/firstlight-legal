First Light is a Christian Scripture, prayer, and focus app for iPhone. If something isn't working, or you have a question or suggestion, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact

Email us at mckaymarketing.ai@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Subscriptions & billing

First Light Plus is billed through your Apple Account. To manage, cancel, or check a subscription, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

App & screen-time protection

The Protect Your Attention feature uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Your app selections never leave your device. If blocking ever behaves unexpectedly, you can adjust or turn it off any time inside First Light, or review permissions under Settings › Screen Time.

Your data

Your prayers, reflections, and journal stay on your device — we operate no servers and keep no copy. See the Privacy Policy for details.
